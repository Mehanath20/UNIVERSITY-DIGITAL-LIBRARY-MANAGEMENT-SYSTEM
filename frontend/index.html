WORKFLOW

How the Digital Library Operates

Four simple steps from catalog discovery to automated return and fine tracking

01
Search & Discover

Search thousands of academic volumes by title, author, or ISBN. Check real-time shelf availability instantly.

02
Borrow or Hold

Borrow available copies within your membership allowance, or place an automated FIFO reservation hold.

03
Research & Study

Enjoy standard loan periods (14 days for students, 30 days for faculty) with timely automated due-date notifications.

04
Return & Settle

Circulation desk processes returns seamlessly. Accrued overdue fines are transparently tallied with 1-click settlement.

INSTITUTIONAL EXCELLENCE

Engineered for Academic Rigor

Designed from the ground up to replace outdated manual paper registers with an auditable, modern digital platform tailored for university faculties and students.

Strict Role-Based Security

Fine-grained permissions guaranteeing separate authorized consoles for Students, Faculty, Librarians, and Administrators.

Automated Reservation FIFO Queue

High-demand books trigger priority hold notifications the instant a copy is checked back into circulation.

Comprehensive Institutional Reports

Executive dashboards tracking copy depreciation, lost copies, most-borrowed curriculum titles, and fine balances.
